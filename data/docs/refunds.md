# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## How to request a refund
Open the order in your account, choose Return Item, and select a reason. A return label is emailed within one business day.

## Processing time
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received.
