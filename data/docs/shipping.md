# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Tracking
Every shipment includes a tracking number, emailed when the order leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Delayed orders
If an order has not arrived within 7 days after the estimated delivery date, contact support with the order number so we can open a carrier investigation.
